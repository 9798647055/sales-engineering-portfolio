# Sales Playbook: Automated Collection Reminder Calls for Lenders

Reusable notes for banks, NBFCs, fintechs and other lenders.

## Elevator pitch
"Your agents should not spend their day reminding people about due dates. We call every customer automatically when a payment is due, and tell your agents exactly who did not pick up, so their calls go where they matter."

## Ideal customer
- Banks, NBFCs and fintech lenders with large loan portfolios
- Collections teams that call customers manually for routine reminders
- Lenders that need visibility into which customers were reached

## Discovery questions
1. How many loan accounts need a reminder each month?
2. How far ahead of the due date do you remind, and do you call after the due date?
3. How are reminders done today: agents, SMS, or both?
4. Where does the due-date data live, and can it trigger a call request?
5. Do your agents need to see call results in your own portal?
6. What happens today when the customer does not answer?
7. Which languages do your customers speak?
8. Which rules apply to your collection calls (calling hours, consent, number series)?
9. How many agents handle follow-up?

## Value proposition
| Client pain | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Agents spend time on routine reminders | Automated outbound calls |
| No clear list of customers not reached | Automatic not-picked-up report |
| Reports sit in one system | Report pushed to your own portal as well |
| Follow-up is inconsistent | Agents get a ready list to call manually |
| Process change is hard | Calls are triggered from your existing portal |

## Likely objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Will customers be bothered by automated calls?" | Calls follow your timing and message rules, and a polite reminder with amount and date is clear and brief. |
| "Can we see the results in our own system?" | Yes. The report is pushed to your portal. |
| "What if the customer does not answer?" | It appears in the report, and your agent follows up personally. |
| "Does it replace our agents?" | No. It handles the routine first touch so agents focus on the customers who need a conversation. |

## Cross-sell ideas
- Payment link by SMS or WhatsApp after the call
- Keypad options in the call, such as "press 1 to be called back"
- Multi-language reminder calls
- Dialer for the agents' manual follow-up calls
- Call recording and quality monitoring
