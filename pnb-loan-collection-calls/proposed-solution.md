# Proposed Solution

## Positioning
Cloud calling as an **automated first touch for loan collections**: the platform handles routine reminders and hands the cases that need a person to PNB's live agents, with full visibility in both portals.

## What was proposed
1. **Automated outbound reminder calls** to loan account holders, triggered from the PNB portal and placed by the Sarv platform.
2. **Automatic reporting** of calls that were not picked up, in the Sarv portal.
3. **Report push to the PNB online portal**, so live agents see the same information.
4. A **manual follow-up step** by PNB agents for customers who did not pick up.

## How it works
1. A loan payment becomes due, and the **PNB portal** initiates the call request to the Sarv platform.
2. The Sarv platform places an **automated outbound call** to the loan account holder.
3. If the customer picks up, the call **reminds them of the due amount and the date to pay**.
4. If the customer does not pick up, a **report is generated automatically in the Sarv portal**.
5. The same report is **pushed to the PNB online portal**.
6. A **PNB live agent** reviews the report, sees that the customer did not pick up, and **calls the customer manually** to request payment.

## What the client provides
- The PNB portal that initiates the call requests
- Loan due information: customer, amount and date
- Live agents who handle the manual follow-up

## Why this approach
| Reason | Benefit to client |
|---|---|
| Automated first call | Agents are not used for routine reminders |
| Same report in two portals | Shared, consistent view for Sarv and PNB |
| Not-picked-up list for agents | Follow-up effort goes where it is needed |
| Triggered from the PNB portal | Fits the bank's existing workflow |
